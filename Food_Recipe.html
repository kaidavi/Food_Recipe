🍕Classic Beef Lasagna

Lasagna is a popular Italian pasta dish made by layering wide, flat sheets of pasta with different fillings and sauces. A typical lasagna contains layers of pasta ssheets, minced beef cooked in tomato-based sauce, creamy white sauce called bechamel, and grated cheese such as mozzarella or Parmesan. These layers are arranged in a baking dish and then baked in an oven until the pasta is tender and the cheese on top becomes melted and golden. Basically, you can think os lasagna as a layered pasta dish rather than pasta mixed together like spaghetti. 😂

Ingredients

  • Meat sauce
  • White sauce (bechamel)
  • Assembling equipments

Meat Sauce

  • 500g minced beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 400g canned tomatoes, crushed
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1-2 tbsp cooking oil

White sauce (bechamel)

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 and a half cups milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • A little black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)

Instructions

  1. Make the meat sauce

    • Heat oil in a pan and saute the onion until soft.
    • Add garlic and minced beef. Cook until the beef is browned.
    • Add tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt and pepper.
    • Simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occassionally.

  2. Make the bechamel

    • Melt butter in a saucepan.
    • Add flour and stir for about 1 minute.
    • Gradually add milk while whisking continuosly
    • Cook until the sauce becomes smooth and thick.
    • Add salt, pepper and optional nutmeg

  3. Prepare the lasagna sheets

    • If you're using oven-ready/no-boil sheets, use them directly.
    • Otherwise, cook the sheets according to the pacjage instructions.

  4. Assemble In a backing dish, layer:

    • A little meat sauce
    • Lasagna sheets
    • Meat Sauce
    • Bechamel
    • Mozzarella

    Repeat the layers until you've used the ingredients. Finish with bechamel+plenty of cheese on top.

  5. Bake

    • Cover loosely with foil.
    • Bake at 180 degrees about 30 minutes.
    • Remove the foil and bake for another 10-15 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbling.

    Let it sit for 10-15 minutes after removing it from the oven. This helps the layers hold together when you cut it.
